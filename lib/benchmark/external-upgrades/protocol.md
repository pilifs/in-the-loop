# External dependency upgrade evaluation

The unit of measurement is one maintainer decision on one client dependency upgrade. The five disclosed calibrations, the two named BUMP examples, their duplicates and the project's existing fixtures are excluded from both scored sets. The curator committed the randomization seed before any product run. [Execution brief](../../../llm-dlc/loops/external-upgrade-evaluation.md) · [Public seed commitment](seed-commitment.json)

## Selection before the first product report

1. Snapshot the CompSuite registry at `6e6d738c8b0ca6ecbba6220b1fd549b2ae14a05d`, BUMP at `324d5513aa5ca40b5cb32de5b816a58fa60bd7bb` and CompCheck at `937d1d1495c4ff5449443183b70f465d1fe0b4d0`. The cutoff for merged PRs is 2026-11-20 23:00 UTC. Source IDs are `compsuite:<registry id>`, `bump:<record SHA>` or `github:<lowercase owner>/<repo>#<integer PR>`. Keep the complete private candidate list, discovery source, prior exposure, exclusion reason and supporting artifact. Preserve the distinction between an actual PR and a benchmark-curated version pair.
2. Use non-CVE Java/Maven production-library upgrades. Require the same production source on both sides, including generated sources, comparable builds, the ordered resolved classpaths and actual execution of the consumer on each side. Record transitive changes and attribute any difference to the closure until isolated. Exclude a build, linkage, plugin, test-tool, policy or dependency-resolution failure before the consumer observation. A test-only dependency is a calibration or an exclusion. Preserve evidence for every screened candidate, including a passing wrapper that ran zero tests.
3. Treat a client as its upstream repository and a library family as the upstream project that releases the changed artifact. Dedupe by client, family, versions, trigger and shared incident across both sets. Use the exact case-sensitive UTF-8 ID in `SHA256(raw_seed || id)`. Traverse CompSuite in ascending hash order, then BUMP. Admit an independently reproduced functional difference only after old and new reach the same consumer observation. Record a preserving companion wherever feasible. Cap occurrences of each client and library family at two per stratum. Publish a shortfall if these constraints exhaust the available candidates.
4. For each change seek a dependency-only merged PR from the same client and library family first, then another client of the same family, then a matched Java/Maven ecosystem. Search within 365 days either side of the original change date within each tier. Compare the same version-step class (major, minor or patch) and the same build type (single module or reactor) in the ecosystem fallback. Within a tier sort by absolute distance in UTC seconds, then seed rank. Freeze the search results, dates and qualification of each attempted PR. An independent finite oracle or separately justified proof must cover the caller's original scope. If the new side differs, record the discovery, exclude it from preservation, and continue in order.
5. Lock twelve behavioral changes and twelve scoped preservations. Reserve four further changes and four preservations before opening any V0 report. Within each reserve stratum choose the first two qualifying exposures in an entirely new client of a library family in the 24 and the first two in families absent from the 24. Keep separate commitments to the two case lists, artifact identities, signed reference packets and reasons for exclusions. Publish denominators and any pre-lock shortfall. Every locked case keeps its place after a failure, timeout or missing report.

## Operator, review and grading

Use the existing claim design, preparation, verification and handoff workflow at the pinned commit. The two opening examples calibrate intake and adjudication before cohort selection. A fresh operator and claim reviewer receive a neutral pair, ordinary review-time context and the common request. They must be unable to read the curator checkout, full CI reports, excluded trigger or compiled tests, later fixes, dataset history, other operators' reports and the reserved answers. Run full historical CI separately. A person furnishing an observer or domain oracle makes the attempt assisted. Log every edit, minute and clarification.

> Identify which behavior of this application could change for its caller. Check the most consequential preservation or change claim you can justify with the pinned verifier. Record the result as open at the first unsupported operation.

For each attempt record the case ID, revision and artifact digests, claim, observer, input domain, qualifications, workflow and configuration identity, cold/warm state, first actionable evidence, 15-minute checkpoint, final elapsed time, human minutes, token use, compute cost when known, CI baseline and evidence links. Record the original CI trigger and additional value separately. Record the exact unsupported operation for an open result. Preserve the first report before reference reveal. Two independent graders compare the observation, evidence coverage and appropriate review action. Keep both original grades and a written reconciliation of any disagreement.

Use four vCPUs, 16 GiB, two hours from intake including preparation, queue and replay, and at most 15 human review minutes per PR. The cap is 250,000 uncached input plus generated tokens per PR across all model stages, counting reasoning tokens within generated tokens. At 15 minutes the two opening sessions had used 176,898 and 214,213 tokens on that basis. Record cached input and actual dollars separately when available. [Calibration usage and confirmed budget](budget.json) Use the same cap for both revisions of the product. Use separate cold case workspaces, an identical read-only shared tool cache and empty case/proof caches. Duplicate cases and prior reports cannot warm the transfer runs. Record the pinned subject JDK for each case separately from the verifier JDK.

The operational baseline is the complete original client CI on all 24. For CompCheck, record the applicable denominator, normal knowledge, and a second run removing only entries for the same incident and client. Preselect the JBMC comparison as the two lowest seed ranks in each main stratum with an executable matched property and a declared unwind bound. Publish excluded cases, harness effort and unwinding completeness. A hand-supplied engine property earns engine credit only.

Count one relevant change per independently replayed original-scope counterexample, and one relevant preservation per non-vacuous covered original domain. Report native, other proof and exhaustive JVM routes separately. Count any contradicted accepted assurance at the PR level. Count errors about whole-upgrade safety, irrelevant claims, missing reports, false routine alerts and useful findings beyond CI. A narrower or assisted claim retains its technical evidence. Classify each miss by its first consequential cause. Prioritize engineering by distinct PRs affected. Freeze V1 before the reserve opens, run V0 and V1 independently on identical inputs, and publish both-correct, V0-only, V1-only and neither by family.

## Pilot and investment gates

Open the prospective pilot only at eight relevant changes out of twelve, eight scoped preservations out of twelve and zero unresolved contradicted assurances. Label any score obtained by repairing the original 24 as recovery. Count a transfer gain only when matched V0 missed and the untouched case exercised the change. Publish regressions as well.

Each of three consenting teams contributes its first five consecutive eligible upgrades. Record all arrivals, pre-report CI and intended action, delivery time, actual decision, human minutes and voluntary repeat requests in that team's agreed environment. Expansion requires two teams voluntarily choosing another run, a documented improvement beyond CI, and zero unresolved contradicted assurances. Consent and participation must come from maintainers.
