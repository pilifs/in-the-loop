## Pilot and investment gates

Open the prospective pilot only at eight relevant changes out of twelve, eight scoped preservations out of twelve and zero unresolved contradicted assurances. Label any score obtained by repairing the original 24 as recovery. Count a transfer gain only when matched V0 missed and the untouched case exercised the change. Publish regressions as well.

Each of three consenting teams contributes its first five consecutive eligible upgrades. Record all arrivals, pre-report CI and intended action, delivery time, actual decision, human minutes and voluntary repeat requests in that team's agreed environment. Expansion requires two teams voluntarily choosing another run, a documented improvement beyond CI, and zero unresolved contradicted assurances. Consent and participation must come from maintainers.
