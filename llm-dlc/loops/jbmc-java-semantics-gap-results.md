# Java semantics on the native checker and JBMC

The opening checker decided **6/13** historical JBMC-only claims and the final checker decided **13/13**, a gain of **7** at unchanged scope. The final decisions were 4 proofs and 9 refutations replayed on the JVM. The native-only regression controls held at **24/24**, with 17 proofs and 7 replayed refutations. The historical denominator remains 187, with 133 shared successes, 24 native-only, 13 staged-JBMC-only and 17 decided by neither engine.

On the **60** independently registered campaign statements, direct native decisions moved from **12/60** to **50/60**. The final total includes **29 proofs** and **21 original-JVM-replayed refutations**. **38 statements** gained a decision at their original scope across **13 of the 15 semantic families**. There were 0 native regressions. JVM-contradicted native verdicts went from 2 to 0.

Historical runs used the original benchmark’s JDK 25 harness, 120-second deadline, unwind 16, array bound 8 and staged integrated JBMC. New public callers used Temurin 21.0.13, explicit strict engines and the same 120-second deadline. Every linked measurement records the original classpath order, binary and model identity, input bounds and costs. The additional registered positive list-search control sits outside the 13.

## What the public callers showed
